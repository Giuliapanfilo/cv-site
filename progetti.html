Tears of the Campus

Bachelor's thesis

A serious game designed to help new students find their way around university life. The player explores faithful 3D reconstructions of the department's real spaces, modelled in Blender from photos and floor plans, and takes on quests inspired by real procedures: enrolment, library loans, meetings with professors.

At the heart of the gameplay is Enroll Battle, a Pokémon-style turn-based duel against the administrative office, designed to turn bureaucratic anxiety into curiosity. Collectible items scattered across the map add a narrative and motivational layer to the journey.

PokéVision

Knowledge Engineering project

A system to help less experienced players build decks in the Pokémon Trading Card Game. At its core is an OWL ontology of the domain (cards, decks, game zones, rules), validated with SHACL constraints and enriched with SWRL rules that infer the validity of actions and evolutions.

On top of this knowledge base, a recommendation engine uses unsupervised clustering of decks (K-Means, Agglomerative, DBSCAN, BIRCH) to suggest missing cards starting from an incomplete deck, evaluated through masked prediction on real tournament data.
